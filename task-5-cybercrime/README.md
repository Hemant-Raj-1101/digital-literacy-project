UPI # Case Study: UPI Payment Fraud - How College Students Are Targeted


fraud is a common online scam where people are tricked into sending money through apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm. It mainly targets college students because they use digital payments daily but may not always be fully aware of security risks.

This usually starts with a fake call. The scammer pretends to be from a bank or customer support and creates urgency by saying things like your account will be blocked or your KYC is expired. This creates panic and people act without thinking.

Then comes the main trick. The fraudster asks for an OTP, or tells you to scan a QR code or enter a code in your UPI app. In reality, these actions approve a payment, not verification.

Once done, money gets transferred instantly and the scammer disappears. Recovery is very difficult.

For example, a first-year student lost ₹15,000 just by following such instructions in panic.

This shows the importance of staying calm, not sharing OTPs, and always verifying before taking any action.



###prevention
#Stay Safe Online: Prevention

Staying safe online is not difficult, but it needs awareness and small smart habits.

First, always use strong passwords and keep them different for every account. It may feel extra work, but it protects you from big risks. Also, turn on 2FA because it gives extra security even if your password is known.

Before clicking any link, take a moment to check it. Many scams happen just because people click without thinking. Keep your apps and devices updated, as updates fix security issues.

For money safety, never share your OTP with anyone. No bank will ever ask for it. Also, avoid scanning unknown QR codes because they can directly take money from your account. Keep low balance in UPI-linked accounts to reduce risk.

On social media and email, don’t trust random messages or “too good to be true” offers.

If something goes wrong, act fast—call 1930 and inform your bank.
