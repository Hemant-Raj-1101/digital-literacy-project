# Social Media Do's and Don'ts for College Students

## DO's ✅

1. **DO keep your profiles professional**
   - Use a clear, appropriate profile picture
   - Use your real name or professional username
   - Complete your bio with relevant information

2. **DO think before you post**
   - Consider if your future employer would be comfortable seeing this
   - Ask yourself: "Is this helpful? Is this kind? Is this necessary?"
   - Remember that screenshots and saved posts last forever

3. **DO use privacy settings**
   - Review privacy settings regularly
   - Set accounts to private when appropriate
   - Limit who can tag you in posts and photos

4. **DO fact-check before sharing**
   - Verify news from reliable sources before reposting
   - Don't spread misinformation or fake news
   - Use fact-checking websites like Alt News or Boom Live

5. **DO engage positively**
   - Comment constructively and respectfully
   - Support peers with encouraging words
   - Report harmful content instead of engaging with it

---

## DON'Ts ❌

1. **DON'T share personal information publicly**
   - Never post your address, phone number, or Aadhaar details
   - Avoid sharing real-time location
   - Don't post photos of government ID cards or tickets

2. **DON'T post during academic activities**
   - Avoid using phones during class or exams
   - Don't post about exam papers or question papers
   - Respect academic integrity at all times

3. **DON'T engage in online arguments**
   - Don't respond to trolls or negative comments
   - Never post hateful, discriminatory, or offensive content
   - Block and report harassing accounts instead of engaging

4. **DON'T share inappropriate content**
   - No photos with alcohol, drugs, or inappropriate behavior
   - Avoid posting in revealing or unprofessional clothing
   - Don't share content that could embarrass you later

5. **DON'T accept requests from strangers**
   - Only connect with people you know personally or professionally
   - Be cautious of fake profiles and scammers
   - Don't click on suspicious links sent by unknown contacts


## Remember

- **Digital footprint lasts forever** - What you post today can be screenshotted and shared years later
- **Employers check social media** - Many companies review candidates' online presence
- **Think twice, post once** - It's better to not post than to regret posting
- **Report cybercrime** - If you see something wrong, report to cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930
